# Support knowledge base — accepted notification but recipient reports no delivery

**Scope:** General troubleshooting checklist. The case simulation does not model a particular vendor's internal implementation.

1. **Safety and impact:** Is the message still urgent? Confirm appropriate alternative communications and affected scope.
2. **Dispatch vs delivery:** Acceptance status is not recipient receipt. Collect request ID, UTC event time and authorized records.
3. **Confirmation rules:** Determine whether earlier acknowledgement may prevent follow-up channel attempts.
4. **Contact data:** With an authorized administrator, verify path validity, channel preferences and mapping.
5. **Scheduling:** Check broadcast window, retry rules and permitted expiration.
6. **Provider status:** Separate handoff, provider delivery, device observation and user acknowledgement.
7. **Escalation:** Escalate when verified impact, elapsed time and vendor policy justify it; include a redacted timeline, investigations already attempted and a precise question.
8. **Closure:** Clearly identify confirmed facts, remaining uncertainty and any validated customer action.

Avoid posting sensitive contact details, making unverified outage claims, or presenting these fictional timing rules as vendor policy.
