01 / The customer experience

An accepted request is not a delivery receipt.

The simulated team sent a weather alert. One API response cannot establish which delivery attempts happened or whether recipients acknowledged the message. Before troubleshooting, establish current risk, impacted scope, approved fallback communication and the exact record IDs.
